Script format this tool reads

Every spoken step starts with a tag in square brackets. Slide headers set the timeline.

➡️ **SLIDE 2: Title here** *(Minutes 4–7)*

🗣️ **[SPEAK]** **Bold lead-in** then the rest [2]
🗣️ **[OPTIONAL]** **[SPEAK]** Extra background detail (skipped when behind)
❓ **[ASK OMAR]** "Your question?"
🛑 **[PAUSE]** *(Listen: e.g., "likely answer")*
1. Numbered items attach to the line above

Other formats work too: plain paragraphs, Markdown headings (#, ##), headings like "Part 1: …" or "Section 2 (0–5 min)", ALL-CAPS headings, dialogue labels (Teacher:, Ask:, Student:, Pause:), "(pause)" notes and lines ending with "?" (treated as questions with an automatic listen pause). They are converted automatically on load.

Keys on a computer: Space, Enter or → for Next. ← for Back. T starts/pauses timer. F toggles focus. C toggles camera column. V toggles voice follow.
